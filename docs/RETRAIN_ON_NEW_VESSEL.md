# Retraining on a new vessel

The shared G13 checkpoint was trained on one procedural aortic-arch geometry. It is not evidence of generalization to a different phantom. This page specifies the inputs required before training a new G13 model; it does not certify physical safety.

## Required assets and coordinate frame

Use a watertight or simulator-loadable surface mesh and a YAML centerline file with `BranchWithRadii`-compatible branches (see `configs/scenes/phantom_centerlines_example.yaml`). Every branch needs a unique name, at least two noncoincident 3-D points in millimetres, and one positive lumen radius in millimetres per point. Mesh, centerlines, target coordinates, and insertion point/direction must describe the **same vessel coordinate frame**. The five guidewire tracking points are generated by the simulator, not supplied as training data.

Copy `configs/scenes/phantom_template.yaml` beside your assets and edit `mesh_path`, `centerline_path`, `insertion_position_mm`, `insertion_direction_unit`, target branch names (or manual targets), and projection fields. Relative asset paths resolve against the scene YAML, not the current shell. `target.mode: centerline_random` samples from the named branches each reset; `target.mode: manual` uses `coordinates_mm`. The target threshold is in mm. Ensure sampled targets are reachable and lie in the represented lumen. The registration block applies an **explicit** uniform scale, then Y/Z/X rotations in degrees, then an XYZ translation in mm to mesh, centerlines, insertion position, and manual targets. Radii scale uniformly. The insertion direction rotates but does not translate or scale. Set identity registration when the assets are already aligned. No size or orientation is inferred automatically; anisotropic scaling is intentionally unsupported because a scalar radius would become ambiguous. Verify the transformed mesh and centerlines overlap before training.

`image_rot_zx_deg`, `image_center_mm`, and optional `field_of_view_mm` define the simulated 2-D projection. A cropped field of view can invalidate point alignment; the sanity check must pass. The action remains the original J-shaped wire's normalized `[translation, rotation]` mapped to ±50 mm/s and ±3.14 rad/s. SOFA friction, 0.006 s step, and 22 substeps per RL action remain frozen.

## Exact 22-D observation

Indices 0–9 are five ordered, normalized 2-D tracking points `p0..p4` (two coordinates each); 10–11 are normalized target 2-D coordinates; 12–13 are sine/cosine of device rotation. The remaining eight values use only **current** guidewire/vessel geometry, never force or future state:

- `d0_normalized`, `d1_normalized`, `d2_normalized` (14–16): for the tip and the next two distal samples at 1 mm projected spacing, take the maximum over centerline segments of `interpolated_radius − 3D_distance_to_segment`, then `tanh(clearance_mm/20 mm)`. These are signed centerline-radius **lumen proxies, not exact wall distances**. Positive is inside at least one represented tube.
- `sin_delta_theta`, `cos_delta_theta` (17–18): the signed 2-D angle between the projected tangent of the tip's selected lumen segment and the distal guidewire direction `p0−p1`; neutral `(0,1)` when direction is invalid.
- `kappa_distal_normalized` (19): angle between `p0−p1` and `p1−p2`, divided by π. It is a bend-angle proxy, not physical curvature per unit length.
- `r_local_normalized` (20): `tanh(local_radius_mm/20 mm)` for the selected tip segment.
- `direction_valid` (21): 1 only when both distal-wire and projected-vessel direction norms exceed `1e-6`, otherwise 0.

The first three points are paired with simulator 3-D points using the frozen 1 mm projected-point selection. Geometry extraction checks their alignment against the original tracking observation to `1e-4 mm`. The remaining two original tracking points are retained in the base observation; they do not directly enter the clearance query. `NormalizeTracking2DEpisode` supplies the original 2-D coordinate normalization. Keep its resulting scale/fov assumptions in mind when changing vessel dimensions: the model's input is still numerically 22-D, but its distribution may shift substantially.

## Before training

Run `python scripts/check_new_vessel.py --scene-config /path/to/phantom_scene.yaml`. It validates asset loading, branch radii, target, five tracking points, finite 22-D observation and clearance/radius features, direction validity, frozen action limits, reset, one zero-action step, and 22-substep distal-force extraction. Inspect its reported target, clearance, local radius, and tracking alignment. Visual/physical inspection of mesh–centerline registration and target reachability is still required; a one-step check cannot establish anatomy fidelity or navigability.

Then use [TRAINING.md](TRAINING.md). Retraining changes the vessel-specific data distribution; it does not alter the frozen G13 network family or G16.1 safety filter.
